## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-11-02

First public beta.

### Added

- `j` / `k`, `gg` / `G` through the sidebar, messages, threads and search results.
- `H` / `L` move between areas on screen.
- `Enter` opens a channel, thread or search result; on a message without
  replies it starts a thread in its reply box.
- `y` copies a message or reply link; `i` types; `x` closes the thread.
- `/` filters the sidebar or searches the open channel.
- The cursor uses Slack's own hover look.
